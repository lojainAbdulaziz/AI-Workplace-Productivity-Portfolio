## Project Overview

This portfolio demonstrates the practical use of Generative AI for workplace productivity in the role of a Project Coordinator. The portfolio is built around a consistent workplace scenario in which a supplier has delayed the delivery of a product required for an organizational project.

Generative AI is used to support professional communication, information processing, project planning, verification, responsible AI use, and future workplace integration. The portfolio demonstrates a human-in-the-loop approach in which AI supports drafting and organization while the human remains responsible for verification, decisions, refinement, and final approval.

The overall workflow is:

**Organize → Generate → Verify → Refine → Human Sign-off**

## Technical Documentation

### Prompt Engineering Approach

The portfolio uses structured prompting techniques practiced during the training program.

The C.A.R.E. framework is used to define:

- **Context**
- **Action**
- **Role**
- **Expected Output**

The R.C.T.O. framework is also used where leading with the appropriate role and defining the task structure is more effective.

Prompts are designed to provide clear context, define the requested action, establish the AI role, specify the expected output, and prevent the invention of missing information.

---

### Writing Workflow

The professional writing workflow follows four stages:

**Draft → Verify → Refine → Human Sign-off**

AI can assist with creating and refining a draft, but the content must be checked against the available information before it is used. The final communication requires human review and approval.

---

### Information Workflow

The information-processing workflow follows:

**Input → Prompt → AI Output → Human Review → Final Structured Output**

The workflow converts unstructured workplace information into a structured format while preserving uncertainty and avoiding unsupported assumptions.

Missing information is marked as:

`[Not Specified]`

rather than being invented.

---

### Planning Workflow

The planning workflow follows:

**Goal → Mechanisms → Phases → Tasks**

Dependencies and decision points are identified where relevant. Deadlines, owners, commitments, and other details are not invented when they have not been provided or confirmed.

---

### Verification Approach

AI-generated outputs are treated as drafts until they have been checked against the available source information.

The verification approach checks:

- Source accuracy
- Dates and figures
- Names and roles
- Uncertainty
- Unsupported or invented information
- Completeness
- Audience and tone
- Human approval

The verification checklist is also applied to an actual AI-generated example in the portfolio.

---

### Responsible AI Approach

Responsible AI use is addressed through six concepts:

1. Accuracy
2. Accountability
3. Confidentiality
4. Privacy
5. Bias
6. Human Oversight

Information sensitivity is also considered using the:

**Green → Amber → Red**

classification.

## Key Learnings

1. Clear and structured prompts produce more useful, consistent, and controllable AI outputs.
2. AI-generated content should be treated as a draft and verified before being used as workplace information.
3. Missing information should be identified clearly using `[Not Specified]` instead of being guessed or invented.
4. Human judgment remains essential for important workplace decisions, communications, and approvals.
5. Generative AI can improve productivity by helping organize information, draft communications, structure plans, and identify actions.
6. Responsible AI requires attention to accuracy, accountability, confidentiality, privacy, bias, and human oversight.

---

## Responsible Use Statement

This portfolio uses a fictional supplier-delay workplace scenario and does not contain real confidential, private, personal, or sensitive organizational information.

Generative AI is used as a productivity support tool and not as a replacement for human judgment or accountability. AI-generated content was reviewed before inclusion in the portfolio, and important workplace outputs should be verified and approved by a human before being shared or used for decisions.

No missing dates, owners, reasons, figures, commitments, or other details are intentionally invented. When information is unavailable, it is identified as `[Not Specified]`.

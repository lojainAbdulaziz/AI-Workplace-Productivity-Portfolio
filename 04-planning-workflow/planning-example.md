# Planning Workflow

## Scenario

**Role:** Project Coordinator

**Scenario:** A supplier has delayed the delivery of a product required for an organizational project.

## Goal

Manage the supplier delivery delay and minimize its potential impact on the project while keeping decisions based on confirmed information.

---

## 1. Goal

Maintain project continuity by confirming the supplier situation, assessing the impact, communicating the situation to stakeholders, and preparing recovery options.

---

## 2. Mechanisms

The following mechanisms will be used:

- Confirm the revised supplier delivery information.
- Assess the impact on project activities and dependencies.
- Communicate confirmed information to relevant stakeholders.
- Identify recovery options.
- Monitor the situation until the delivery is confirmed.

---

## 3. Phases

### Phase 1 — Confirm

**Purpose:** Establish the confirmed supplier information.

**Tasks:**
- Confirm the revised delivery date.
- Confirm the reason for the delay if available.
- Record any information that remains pending.

**Dependency:** Recovery planning depends on receiving the revised delivery information.

**Decision Point:** Determine whether enough confirmed information is available to assess the project impact.

---

### Phase 2 — Assess

**Purpose:** Understand how the delay may affect the project.

**Tasks:**
- Identify project activities dependent on the delayed product.
- Assess potential schedule or operational impact.
- Separate confirmed impacts from potential risks.

**Dependency:** The assessment depends on the confirmed delivery information and project dependencies.

**Decision Point:** Decide whether the current plan can continue or whether recovery options are required.

---

### Phase 3 — Align and Communicate

**Purpose:** Ensure relevant stakeholders have a clear and accurate update.

**Tasks:**
- Prepare a concise project update.
- Communicate confirmed facts.
- Identify information still pending.
- Highlight actions requiring attention.

**Decision Point:** Confirm that the communication is accurate and appropriate for the audience before sending.

---

### Phase 4 — Recover

**Purpose:** Reduce the potential impact of the supplier delay.

**Tasks:**
- Identify practical recovery options.
- Compare the available options.
- Select an option based on confirmed project information and stakeholder decisions.
- Update the project plan if required.

**Dependency:** Recovery actions depend on the confirmed impact and available options.

**Decision Point:** Select the recovery approach that best supports project continuity.

---

### Phase 5 — Monitor

**Purpose:** Continue tracking the supplier situation until the delivery is confirmed.

**Tasks:**
- Monitor supplier updates.
- Record changes to the delivery status.
- Reassess project impact when new information becomes available.
- Communicate significant changes to stakeholders.

---

## Planning Chain Summary

**Goal → Mechanisms → Phases → Tasks**

The plan does not invent specific deadlines, owners, delivery dates, or supplier commitments. These should only be added when confirmed by the relevant source or decision-maker.

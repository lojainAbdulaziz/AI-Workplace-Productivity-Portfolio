# Verification Checklist

## Scenario

**Role:** Project Coordinator

**Scenario:** A supplier has delayed the delivery of a product required for an organizational project.

## Purpose

The purpose of this checklist is to verify AI-generated workplace outputs before they are used or shared.

---

## Verification Checklist

Before accepting an AI-generated output, I check the following:

- [ ] **Source accuracy:** Does the output accurately reflect the information in the original source?
- [ ] **Dates and figures:** Are all dates, numbers, and deadlines supported by the source?
- [ ] **Names and roles:** Are names, owners, and responsibilities confirmed rather than assumed?
- [ ] **Uncertainty:** Are unknown or unconfirmed details clearly identified?
- [ ] **No invention:** Has the AI added any reason, commitment, impact, or other detail that was not provided?
- [ ] **Completeness:** Does the output include all important information required for the task?
- [ ] **Audience and tone:** Is the output appropriate for the intended workplace audience?
- [ ] **Human approval:** Has a human reviewed and approved the final output before it is used?

---

# Worked Verification Example

## Output Being Verified

The following AI-generated output was created from the supplier-delay scenario:

> The supplier will deliver the product next Tuesday because the shipment was delayed by customs. Sarah will monitor the delivery and the project will be delayed by two days.

---

## Applying the Checklist

| Verification Check | Finding | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Source accuracy | The source only confirms that delivery is delayed. | Fail |
| Dates and figures | "Next Tuesday" and "two days" were not provided. | Fail |
| Names and roles | "Sarah" was not provided as an owner. | Fail |
| Uncertainty | The output presents unconfirmed information as fact. | Fail |
| No invention | Customs delay was not stated in the source. | Fail |
| Completeness | The output does not identify what information is still pending. | Needs improvement |
| Audience and tone | The wording is generally professional but contains unsupported claims. | Needs improvement |
| Human approval | The output should not be approved in its current form. | Fail |

---

## Verification Result

The AI output should **not** be used because it contains multiple invented details:

- An unconfirmed delivery date
- An unsupported reason for the delay
- An invented owner
- An unsupported project delay duration

---

## Corrected Output

A safer version is:

> The supplier has confirmed that the delivery has been delayed. The revised delivery date and reason for the delay have not yet been confirmed. The project impact requires further assessment once the revised delivery information becomes available.

---

## Final Decision

**Decision: Do not use the original AI output.**

The corrected version is preferred because it preserves confirmed information and clearly identifies what remains unknown.

---

## Verification Principle

AI output should be treated as a draft until it has been checked against the source, tested against appropriate criteria, reviewed by a human, and approved for use.

**Identify → Set Criteria → Test → Confirm, Document, and Decide**

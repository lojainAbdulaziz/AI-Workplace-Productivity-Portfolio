# Responsible AI Use Checklist

## Scenario

**Role:** Project Coordinator

**Scenario:** A supplier has delayed the delivery of a product required for an organizational project.

## Purpose

This section explains how Generative AI can be used responsibly in the supplier-delay scenario while maintaining human accountability and protecting sensitive information.

---

# Six Responsible AI Concepts

## 1. Accuracy

AI-generated information must be checked against the original supplier update or other reliable sources. Dates, reasons, project impacts, and commitments must not be accepted without verification.

---

## 2. Accountability

The project coordinator remains responsible for the final communication, decision, and use of AI-generated content. Using AI does not transfer responsibility to the AI system.

---

## 3. Confidentiality

Confidential supplier information, contract terms, internal project information, or other restricted business information should not be entered into an AI system or published in a public GitHub repository unless appropriate authorization and controls are in place.

---

## 4. Privacy

Personal information should not be included unnecessarily. Names, contact details, employee information, customer information, or other personal data should be removed or anonymized when it is not required for the task.

---

## 5. Bias

AI outputs should be reviewed for unsupported assumptions or unfair conclusions. For example, the project coordinator should not assume that a supplier, team member, or department is responsible for a delay without evidence.

---

## 6. Human Oversight

AI can help draft, organize, summarize, and structure information, but a human must review important outputs before they are communicated or used for decisions.

---

# Green / Amber / Red Information Classification

| Information Item | Classification | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Generic statement that a required product delivery is delayed | Green | It is a generic, non-sensitive scenario statement with no identifying or confidential details. |
| Supplier name combined with non-public delivery commitments | Amber | This could reveal business information that should be handled carefully and only used when authorized. |
| Employee or customer personal information, or confidential contract terms | Red | This may contain private or sensitive information and should not be included in a public portfolio or shared with AI without appropriate authorization and controls. |

---

# Responsible Use Rules

1. Use fictional or anonymized workplace examples in the portfolio.
2. Do not upload confidential supplier, company, employee, or customer information.
3. Verify AI-generated facts before using them.
4. Never allow AI to invent missing dates, owners, reasons, figures, or commitments.
5. Keep a human responsible for important decisions and final communications.
6. Use the minimum information necessary to complete the task.

---

# Portfolio Safety Statement

This portfolio uses a fictional supplier-delay scenario and does not contain real confidential, private, personal, or sensitive organizational information.

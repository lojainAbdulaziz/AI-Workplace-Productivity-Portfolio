# Professional Writing Workflow

## Scenario

**Role:** Project Coordinator

**Scenario:** A supplier has delayed the delivery of a product required for an organizational project.

**Writing Goal:** Create a professional internal communication that explains the supplier delay clearly, separates confirmed information from information that still needs confirmation, and identifies the required next actions.

---

## Step 1 — Draft

**Draft Email:**

Subject: Supplier Delivery Delay – Project Update

Dear Team,

I would like to inform you that the supplier has delayed the delivery of the product required for the project.

The revised delivery date and the reason for the delay have not yet been confirmed. The potential impact on the project also requires further assessment.

Please monitor the supplier update and confirm the revised delivery information when it becomes available.

Best regards,  
Project Coordinator

---

## Step 2 — Verify

Before refining the message, the information was checked against the available scenario.

| Information | Verification Result |
|---|---|
| Affected product | Confirmed that a required product is affected |
| Supplier | Supplier is involved, but no specific supplier name is provided |
| Original delivery date | [Not Specified] |
| Revised delivery date | [Not Specified] |
| Reason for delay | [Not Specified] |
| Project impact | Requires further assessment |
| Required team action | Confirm the revised delivery information and assess the impact |

### Verification Notes

- No supplier name was invented.
- No delivery date was invented.
- No reason for the delay was assumed.
- No specific project delay was claimed without confirmation.
- Missing information was marked as [Not Specified].

---

## Step 3 — Refine

After verification, the message was refined to make it clearer, more professional, and more actionable while keeping only supported information.

**Final Email:**

**Subject: Supplier Delivery Delay – Project Update**

Dear Team,

I would like to provide an update regarding the delivery of the product required for the project. The supplier has confirmed that the delivery has been delayed.

The revised delivery date and reason for the delay have not yet been confirmed. The project impact therefore requires further assessment once the revised delivery information is available.

**Required actions:**
- Confirm the revised delivery date when it becomes available.
- Assess the project impact based on the confirmed delivery date.
- Update the project plan if required.

Please note that no additional dates, causes, or commitments have been assumed.

Best regards,  
Project Coordinator

---

## Step 4 — Human Sign-off

Before sending the final message, the project coordinator should complete a human review.

### Human Sign-off Checklist

- [x] Confirm that the supplier delay is accurately represented.
- [x] Confirm that no dates or reasons were invented.
- [x] Confirm that missing information is clearly identified.
- [x] Confirm that the message is appropriate for the internal project team.
- [x] Confirm that the required actions are clear.
- [ ] Final human approval before sending.

---

## Workflow Summary

The writing workflow follows four steps:

**Draft → Verify → Refine → Human Sign-off**

Generative AI can support the drafting and refinement process, but the final communication should be reviewed and approved by a human before it is sent.
